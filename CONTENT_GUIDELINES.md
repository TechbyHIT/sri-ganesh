# Content guidelines

Write for the customer in clear Indian English. One page, one search intent.

Use the service, city, and locality copy that is already reviewed. Do not generate a page by swapping a city name. Do not reorder the same paragraphs and call that unique.

Verified local notes already on a location record may be used. Do not add population, apartment counts, weather, landmarks, warranties, years in business, or job counts that are not in the source data.

Pricing copy is fixed:

“Pricing depends on measurements, material grade, required spacing, installation complexity, building height, site accessibility and total project quantity.”

Do not show a fixed price.

Word targets live in `src/config/publishing.ts`. A short useful page stays in review rather than being padded. The current catalogue drafts are below those targets, so they are not database-published.

Empty projects, testimonials, gallery, and blog pages stay noindex until real items exist.
